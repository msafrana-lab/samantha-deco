Before you write

Three quick answers

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes, always. The first message, my reply and the exchange that follows cost nothing and commit you to nothing. Billing only starts when you book a package, clearly announced beforehand.

I don't know which package to choose. Is that a problem?

Not at all — guiding you is part of my job. Simply describe your situation; I will tell you frankly whether an Advice Visit is enough or whether your project deserves more.

I live on the Swiss side: what changes?

Nothing essential: I regularly work from Geneva to Montreux. A travel fee applies depending on distance, and your quote can be drawn up in Swiss francs. Everything is announced before you decide.
