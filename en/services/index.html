N° 2 · The transformation

Full Project

from €890 ≈ CHF 840 · per room · decreasing rate from the 2nd room · whole houses on quote

This is the most complete package. I design your room from A to Z and show it to you first, in visualisations faithful to your volumes and your light. You approve, we fine-tune, and we only buy after that.

  • Meeting, measurements and a precise brief of your habits and budget
  • Mood board: artistic direction, materials, palette
  • Visualisations of your transformed room, with two rounds of adjustments included
  • Dimensioned layout plan and a costed shopping list, from sofa to vase
  • Coordination of partner tradespeople and follow-up (optional, project-dependent)
  • Final staging day: installation, styling, finishing touches (optional)

Furniture and works budget not included: you buy everything yourself, at public prices, with no hidden margin on my side. And from the second room of the same project, my fees drop by 10%.

N° 3 · The showcase

Home Staging

Flash €390 ≈ CHF 370 · diagnosis + action plan

Full from €990 ≈ CHF 940 · diagnosis + staging carried out

Buyers make up their minds within the first minutes of a viewing. Home staging is about helping them picture themselves right away. I work with private sellers and with estate agencies.

Frequently asked

Everything people ask about pricing

Is travel included?

Yes, within 30 km of Évian-les-Bains (Thonon and the whole Chablais included). Beyond that (Geneva, Nyon, Lausanne, Montreux), a travel fee is added to the quote. You know it before you decide.

I live in Switzerland: can the quote be in francs?

Of course. The CHF amounts shown here are indicative; your quote is drawn up directly in Swiss francs at the day's rate, and that amount is binding.

Are furniture and works included in the prices?

No, and that is a choice. My fees pay for design and guidance. Your purchases are made at public prices, directly with retailers and tradespeople. You keep control of every expense.

How soon can a project start?

An Advice Visit is usually scheduled within 2 weeks. A Full Project starts within 2 to 4 weeks depending on the season, and lasts roughly 4 to 8 weeks from design to staging. The exact pace depends on your availability and furniture lead times.

What if I don't like the visualised result?

Visualisation exists exactly for this. Two rounds of adjustments are included, and nothing is purchased until you truly like the result. You never spend blind.
