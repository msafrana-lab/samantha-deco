The method

How we work together

Meet & listen

We meet at your home or by video call. You tell me about your habits, your wishes and your budget. The whole project starts from there.

Design & visualise

I prepare the mood board, the layout plan and the visualisations of your rooms. We adjust until you say "that's exactly it".

Select & shop

You receive a precise, costed shopping list, with trusted tradespeople if needed. You know where every euro goes before spending it.

Stage & style

I install, style and settle the last details. All that is left is to come home and enjoy it.
