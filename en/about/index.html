About

I learned this job by the lake

Samantha, founder of Rives Intérieures

I learned interior design in homes, not in a school. First my family's, then friends' homes, then their friends', because word of mouth travels fast around here. Dozens of interiors later, my passion became my profession, with a real method, trusted tradespeople and good suppliers on both sides of the border.

When you learn on your own, you observe everything. Why this living room breathes and that one does not. What the lake light does to colours at 8 am and at 7 pm. Why a freshly renovated room can still feel cold, while three good choices are sometimes enough to change everything.

I work across the Lake Geneva basin: Évian apartments that ignore their view, Chablais houses full of potential, properties to showcase around Geneva and Lausanne. I love this territory, its light, its blend of stone, wood and water. And my favourite moment is when a client walks back in and says "this is exactly us".

Samantha

My convictions

Three principles that never change

I.

Listening before style

My job is to bring out your universe, not to install mine in your home. The initial questionnaire is long, on purpose: everything understood well at the start saves corrections at the end.

II.

Budget is a material

You can do something beautiful on a controlled budget. I allocate every euro, mix price ranges freely, and tell you honestly when an expense is not worth it.

III.

See before you do

No major purchase without an approved visualisation. You discover your transformed room on screen, we adjust together, and the money is only spent after that.
